Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Chrono Tracker ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use our mobile application (the "Chrono Tracker").

By using the App, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the information reasonably necessary to provide and improve the App.

1.1 Email Address

If you create an account or otherwise provide your email address, we may collect and process your email address.

We use your email address to:

  • Create and manage your account;
  • Authenticate your account;
  • Provide account-related communications;
  • Respond to support requests;
  • Send important service or security notifications; and
  • Your mail address is not displayed to other users.

We do not sell your email address to third parties.

1.2 Profile Avatar

We store image files you upload as profile avatars.

We use your profile avatar to:

  • Display your profile picture within the App;
  • Help other users recognize your account when you participate in features where your profile is visible; and
  • Provide and operate social or community features of the App.

Your profile avatar is displayed to other users by default. You may hide your profile avatar to other users by going to Settings → Hide Account.

1.3 Country

We collect your country.

Your country may be used to:

  • Provide an appropriate experience based on your location;
  • Display localized list of users;
  • Understand the geographic distribution of our users; and
  • Produce aggregated statistics and analytics about use of the App.

Your country is displayed to other users by default. You may hide your country to other users by going to Settings → Hide Location.

We collect your country at the level described above and do not intend to collect your precise physical location through this feature.

1.4 Study and Time-Tracking Data

Because the App is designed to help you track your study time, we collect information relating to your use of the study timer.

This may include:

  • Study session start and end times;
  • Duration of study sessions;
  • Breaks or other timer-related information;
  • Study topics, sessions, or statistics that you choose to create and/or join;
  • Dates associated with study sessions; and
  • Other information you voluntarily enter into the App relating to your study activity.

We use this information to provide the App's core functionality, including recording, displaying, and analyzing your study time and progress.

Your study and time tracking data are displayed to other users by default. You may hide your study and time tracked data to other users by going to Settings → Hide Account.

1.5 IP Address

When you use the App, our servers or service providers may temporarily receive or log your IP address as part of normal internet communications, security processes, and server operations.

We do not store your IP address as part of your user profile or retain it in our application database.

Where an IP address is temporarily processed by our infrastructure or service providers, it may exist in transient technical logs for a limited period as necessary for security, troubleshooting, fraud prevention, or reliable operation of our services.

2. How We Use Your Information

We may process the information described above for the following purposes:

  • To provide and operate the App;
  • To create and manage user accounts;
  • To save and display your study history;
  • To calculate study-time statistics and progress;
  • To provide customer support;
  • To maintain the security and reliability of the App;
  • To detect and prevent abuse, fraud, or unauthorized access;
  • To diagnose technical problems and improve the App;
  • To communicate with you about your account and the App;
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations; and
  • To conduct aggregated and/or anonymized analytics.

We will not use your personal information for purposes incompatible with those described in this Privacy Policy without providing additional notice where required by applicable law.

6. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, unless a longer period is required or permitted by law.

  • Email address: Retained while your account remains active and for a reasonable period afterward where necessary for legitimate business or legal purposes.
  • Country: Retained while your account remains active and for a reasonable period afterward where necessary for legitimate business or legal purposes.
  • Study-time data: Retained while your account remains active and for a reasonable period afterward where necessary for legitimate business or legal purposes.
  • Profile Avatar: Retained while your account remains active, where avatar deletion is not requested and for a reasonable period afterward where necessary for legitimate business or legal purposes.
  • IP addresses: We do not intentionally store IP addresses in our user database. Any temporary technical logs containing IP addresses are retained only for 30 days or as otherwise necessary for security and technical operations.

When information is no longer needed, we will delete it or anonymize it where reasonably practicable, subject to applicable legal obligations.
